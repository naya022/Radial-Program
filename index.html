The Context

What Radial is facing right now

This program was designed around Radial's documented, live challenges — not a generic L&D template. Every phase maps directly to a real organizational problem.

⚠
Inadequate training — documented by employees
Indeed reviews cite training cut from 3 weeks to 7 days, leaving employees unprepared for complex roles in fulfillment and fraud prevention.
Critical — Immediate
🔄
Rebrand to Paxon creating workforce uncertainty
Radial is actively transitioning to Paxon in 2026 while integrating with European sister companies. Employees report unclear company direction.
Active — Ongoing
📦
7,000+ seasonal hires require rapid onboarding
Annual peak season demands massive, fast onboarding with compliance tracking — with an average employee tenure of just 3.5 years.
Cyclical — Q3/Q4
📋
34 WARN Act filings — 6,100+ workers affected
Significant layoffs across 8 states require strategic reskilling programs to redeploy institutional talent rather than lose it entirely.
Workforce Risk
🤖
AI & automation adoption gap
Radial's own research shows only 6% of consumers have used AI commerce — internally, employees need training to work alongside new automation tools.
Strategic — 2026
🏆
New CEO + diversified growth strategy
Tom Schmitt joined March 2025 to drive customer base diversification. Commercial teams need upskilling on new verticals and the Fast Track product.
Growth Enabler

Skill gap assessment

Structured surveys and manager interviews across all fulfillment center roles and corporate functions. Identifies top 5 skill gaps per role family to prioritize program investment.

LMS platform audit

Full evaluation of Workday and Rippling LMS setup — completion rates, reporting accuracy, user experience, content gaps, and compliance tracking integrity.

Paxon transition impact map

Maps which roles are most affected by the rebrand. Identifies exactly what employees need to know, feel, and do differently — so training is targeted, not generic.

📋

Compliance training inventory

Audits all existing compliance content — delinquency rates, outdated modules, and tracking gaps. Completed before Q4 hiring surge to protect audit readiness.
